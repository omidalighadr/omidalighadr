## Focus
- **AI for research and education.** Structured prompting, literature review and academic-writing
  workflows, taught to ~52K on Instagram and ~2K on Telegram (Karbalad).
- **Python tooling for Persian content.** Typography, automation and research utilities
  built for right-to-left, Persian-script output.

## Flagship: Samery, a single-user AI assistant on Telegram
~17.6K lines of Python, 50+ commands, 9 background workers. Source is private; walkthrough on request.

**Research tools built to avoid hallucination**
- *Reference checker.* Matches in-text citations against the reference list and verifies entries
  through CrossRef and DOI. No claim is generated by a language model, and "not found" is never
  reported as "fake" (books and many Persian journals are simply not indexed).
- *Journal finder.* Suggests a journal only when papers on a similar topic were actually
  published there (CrossRef and DOAJ).

**Content and productivity**
- Persian cover engine: correct Persian shaping, WCAG-checked colour system, layouts that respect
  Instagram's 3:4 grid crop, brand-theme support.
- Office generator: Word, PowerPoint and Excel files from JSON specs.
- Price comparison across Iranian retailers with VPN-aware routing and price alerts.
- Pluggable LLM layer: Gemini or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint, with fallback models.

**Engineering**
Python, python-telegram-bot, asyncio workers, SQLite, Pillow with Raqm, yt-dlp, ffmpeg.
Design rules: a single authorised user, secrets only in the environment, and every external lookup
degrades gracefully and says what it could not verify.
